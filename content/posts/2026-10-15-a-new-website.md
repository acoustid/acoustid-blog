The AcoustID website has a new design. It should be easier to read and easier to get around, and a few pages can now do things they could not before.

Some of what is new:

 - Fingerprints are drawn on the track pages, so you can see at a glance which ones look alike.
 - Tracks with a handful of fingerprints show how similar each pair is.
 - The fingerprint comparison page is interactive: it lines two fingerprints up for you, shows where their bits disagree, and lets you adjust the alignment yourself. Pick two fingerprints on a track page to go straight there.
 - The statistics and about pages have live numbers and charts.
 - The API key page is now an account page.

If you find something broken, or something that used to be easier than it is now, please tell us. Bug reports and suggestions are both welcome on the [AcoustID forum][] or at [info@acoustid.org][].
